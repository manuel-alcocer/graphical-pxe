How it works

When a computer boots from the network, iPXE sends its MAC address, model and serial number. The controller looks up its group and builds a menu with only the images that group allows.

Pick a group on the left to edit it, or create a new one and move computers into it from Clients.

MAC addresses can be spoofed: groups decide what is offered, they are not an access control.
